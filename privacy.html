Privacy Policy for Krishi Loksewa Sathi

Last updated: November 2026

Apar Neupane built the Krishi Loksewa Sathi app as a free educational tool for Nepal Agriculture Lok Sewa (PSC) exam preparation.

Information Collection and Use

The app works offline for study notes, mock tests, and typing practice. We do not collect or store your personal identity information on private servers.

Third-Party Services (Google AdMob)

This app uses Google AdMob to display banner advertisements so the study materials remain free for students. Google AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data to show relevant ads in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.

Children's Privacy

This app is intended for students preparing for Public Service Commission examinations and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer via our GitHub repository: Krishi Loksewa Sathi.
